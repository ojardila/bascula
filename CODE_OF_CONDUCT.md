# Code of conduct

Bascula is built for coffee farms and the people who work on them. Everyone
who takes part here (issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions) is expected
to keep it a friendly place to work.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and with people
  writing in a language that is not their first.
- Critique code and ideas, never people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Accept that the maintainers have the final say on what gets merged.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes or language.
- Personal attacks, trolling or deliberately derailing a thread.
- Publishing anyone's private information (including farm or worker data)
  without their permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block anyone who
does not follow this code, on this repository and its other spaces.

To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment
or contact the maintainer
[@ojardila](https://github.com/ojardila) privately. Reports are kept
confidential.

This code is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
